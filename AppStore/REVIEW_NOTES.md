# App Review Notes

## Login

No login is required.

## Network And Privacy

The app has no backend server, no account system, no advertising SDK, no third-party analytics SDK, and no tracking. Core features work offline. StoreKit is used only for in-app purchases.

## AI Behavior

AI features in v1.0 are local template drafts. They do not call an external AI service. Drafts are editable and never sent automatically. The app does not judge talent, rank ability, or label students.

## In-App Purchases

Products:

- `studio.pro.monthly`
- `studio.pro.yearly`
- `studio.pro.lifetime`

Pro unlocks unlimited students, unlimited AI drafts, advanced analytics, batch parent messages, CSV export, bilingual/pro-style PDF output, watermark removal, and advanced tuner/metronome controls.

## Reviewer Flow

1. Open the app and choose the free path from onboarding.
2. Add a student, create a lesson, open the lesson note screen, choose attendance status, write a note, and save.
3. Open the student detail screen to generate a parent-message draft and a learning-summary draft.
4. Open Settings to see Pro-gated CSV export and subscription restore.
5. Open the paywall from Dashboard or Settings to inspect the StoreKit products.
